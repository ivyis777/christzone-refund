Payment & Refund Policy

At Christzone.in, we are committed to providing a secure and convenient shopping experience for Christian products.

1. Payment

  • We accept payments through the payment methods available at checkout.
  • All prices are displayed on the website and applicable taxes or delivery charges will be shown during checkout.
  • Orders are confirmed only after successful payment.
  • If your payment is deducted but your order is not confirmed, please contact us at kamassupplies@gmail.com .

2. Refunds & Cancellations

  • Customers may request cancellation before the order is shipped.
  • Refund requests must be made within 7 days of receiving the product.
  • Products must be unused and in their original condition to qualify for a refund, unless the product is damaged, defective, or incorrectly delivered.
  • Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method.
  • Refund processing times may vary depending on the customer's bank or payment provider.
  • Certain products may be non-refundable. Any such restrictions will be clearly mentioned on the product page.

Contact Us

For payment or refund-related queries, please contact kamassupplies@gmail.com .

Christzone.in reserves the right to modify this policy when necessary.
